Tangy Elote Styled Pizza

I love Elote, I LOVE Pizza. Let's put the best of both worlds together.

Photo Credit: Southern Living Test Kitchen

  • Lime juice
  • Corn kernels (fresh or canned)
  • Valentina (or you favorite hot sauce for Elote)
  • Coarse black pepper
  • Kosher salt
  • Ready-to-bake pizza dough(or you can make your own!)
  • Sriracha ranch (make your own!)
  • Mozzarella
  • Melted unsalted/salted butter
  • (optional) Chili powder or (granulated/finely minced) garlic
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Cotija cheese or queso fresco(you can also substitute for fresh parmesan)

Elote Mix

This is the Elote mix that will be used for the pizza. This preferably, needs to be made at least an hour before assembling the pizza, in order for the corn to have enough time to soak. Personally, I make it the night before for optimal flavor. Also, because if I want a pizza, I don't want to wait an extra hour on top of prep and cook time!
*This mixture can also be refrigerated for up to one week.

  1. Grab a container and mix together the lime juice, Valentina (or choice of hot) sauce, coarse black pepper and Kosher salt.
  2. Add the kernels* to the container.
    *If canned, drain completely. If fresh from cob, shuck and remove hairs prior to adding.
  3. Cover the container and refridgerate until you are ready to assemble the pizza.

Assemble the Pizza

The fun part!

  1. Preheat the oven according to the recommended temperature of your pizza dough.
  2. Take the pizza dough and place it on an oven safe dish.
  3. Put a layer of sriracha ranch sauce as the base on the dough.
  4. Spread mozarella cheese over the sriracha ranch sauce.
  5. (optional) You can create another layer of sriracha ranch and mozzarella cheese for extra gooeyness!
  6. Take scoops of the elote mix(to your liking) and put it on top of the pizza
  7. Follow the cooking instructions for the dough and put the pizza into the oven for the recommended time, until the crust is golden brown, or to your liking

(optional) Yummy Butter

This step is optional, but I love a buttery crust!

  1. Grab a saucepan and heat the butter until melted.
  2. Mix in the chili powder or garlic with the butter and let cook for 1-2 minutes.
  3. (optional) Feel free to add additional ingredients to taste!
  4. Keep the butter mixture warm until the pizza is ready.

Finishing Touches

Once the pizza dough is cooked to your liking, take it out and place it one a rack. It's time to decorate the pizza!

  1. Brush the melted chili/garlic butter on the edges of the pizza fresh out of the oven.
  2. (optional) Top the pizza with the sriracha ranch. (I like to make a swirl design for extra pizzazz!)
  3. Break apart the cilanto and sprinkle it on the pizza.
  4. Break apart the cotija(or cheese topping preference) and sprinkle that on top of the pizza.
  5. Slice pizza and enjoy

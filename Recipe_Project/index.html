Tangy Elote Pizza

I love Elote, I LOVE Pizza. Let's put the best of both worlds together.

Photo Credit: Southern Living Test Kitchen

  • Lime juice
  • Corn Kernels(fresh or canned)
  • Valentina Sauce(or you favorite hot sauce for Elote)
  • Coarse Black Pepper
  • Kosher Salt
  • Ready-to-bake pizza dough(or you can make your own!)
  • Sriracha Ranch (you can make your own easily)
  • Mozzarella
  • Melted Unsalted/Salted butter
  • (Optional) Chili Powder or Gradulated/Finely minced Garlic
  • Fresh Cilantro
  • Cotija Cheese or Queso Fresco(you can also substitute for fresh parmesan)

Elote Mix

This is the Elote mix that will be used for the pizza. This preferably, needs to be made at least an hour before assembling the pizza, in order for the corn to have enough time to soak. Personally, I make it the night before for optimal flavor. Also, because if I want a pizza, I don't want to wait an extra hour on top of prep and cook time!
*This mixture can also be refrigerated for up to one week.

  1. Grab a container and mix together the lime juice, Valentina (or choice of hot) sauce, coarse black pepper and Kosher salt.
  2. Add the kernels* to the container.
    *If canned, drain completely. If fresh from cob, shuck and remove hairs prior to adding.
  3. Cover the container and refridgerate until you are ready to assemble the pizza.

Assemble the Pizza

The fun part!

  1. Take the premade pizza dough and place it on an oven safe dish.
  2. Put a layer of sriracha ranch on the base of the dough.
  3. Shred a whole bunch of mozarella cheese over the sriracha ranch.
  4. (Optional) I put some sriracha ranch over the cheese and then cover it back with cheese creating another layer.
  5. Take a couple scoops the elote topping and put it ontop of the pizza
  6. Put into the oven until the crust is to your liking

Finishing Touches

Once the Pizza is cooked take it out and place it one a rack. It's time to decorate the pizza!

  1. (optional)Create a design on the top of the pizza using the sriracha ranch. I tend to just make a swirl from outside to inside the pizza.
  2. Chop up the cilanto and place it on the pizza
  3. Grate the parmesan on top of the pizza
  4. Slice pizza and enjoy!
